Your Hunger, Our Hustle

At BiteQue, we're more than just a food delivery service — we're your neighbors, friends, and fellow food lovers. Founded in April 2025, our mission is to bridge the gap between local restaurants and communities, ensuring delicious meals are just a tap away.

We support small restaurants with low commission rates and help users order quickly with a smooth, easy-to-use platform. Our riders are the heartbeat of BiteQue — prompt, reliable, and community-first. When you choose BiteQue, you’re supporting local dreams.

We understand the challenges our local eateries face and the hustle of daily life that leaves little time for meal preparation. That's why we've created a platform that not only supports small restaurants by offering them lower commission rates but also provides our customers with a user-friendly interface for quick and easy ordering.

Our dedicated riders are the heartbeat of BiteQue, working tirelessly to deliver your favorite dishes promptly and with care. By choosing BiteQue, you're not just satisfying your hunger—you're supporting local businesses and contributing to a community-driven initiative.

Join us in our journey to make food delivery more personal, efficient, and community-focused. Your hunger fuels our hustle, and together, we can create a more connected and flavorful world.

Our Story

BiteQue was born in April 2025 from a simple truth — our community was hungry, but restaurants were overwhelmed.

So we created BiteQue — a local-first delivery platform that:

  • Provides a fast, easy food ordering experience
  • Supports local eateries with fair commission policies
  • Delivers food fresh, hot, and without delay

Every order placed on BiteQue helps fuel a kitchen, support a rider, and connect people. It’s food delivery — reimagined, for your neighborhood.
