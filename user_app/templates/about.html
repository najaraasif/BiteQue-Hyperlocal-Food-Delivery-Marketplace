About ZemQue

Delivering happiness to your doorstep in Kupwara since 2025. We’re more than just a delivery service – we’re your local connection to fresh flavors.

Our Story

ZemQue was born in the heart of Kupwara with a simple mission: to bring local restaurants and customers closer than ever. We started in April 2025 with just a handful of riders and a dream to make food delivery faster, friendlier, and more community-driven.

Today, we’re proud to connect people with their favorite meals while supporting local businesses and riders.

Our Mission

To deliver delicious meals quickly, reliably, and with a smile – empowering Kupwara’s local food scene.

Our Vision

To become Kupwara’s most trusted delivery service, expanding to nearby towns while keeping our community-first approach.
