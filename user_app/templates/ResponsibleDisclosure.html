Responsible Disclosure

Help us keep ZemQue safe and secure. If you find a vulnerability, we encourage you to report it responsibly.

Security is a top priority at ZemQue. We appreciate the work of security researchers and ethical hackers who help identify vulnerabilities before they can be exploited. This Responsible Disclosure Policy explains how to report issues securely.

1. How to Report a Vulnerability

If you believe you have discovered a security vulnerability in any of our systems, please email us at:
📧 security@zemque.com

When reporting:

  • Provide detailed steps to reproduce the vulnerability.
  • Include any relevant screenshots, code, or logs.
  • Do not publicly disclose the vulnerability before we have resolved it.

2. Guidelines for Responsible Disclosure

  • Do not exploit the vulnerability beyond what is necessary to prove its existence.
  • Do not attempt to access, modify, or delete any data that is not your own.
  • Respect user privacy and avoid any disruption to our services.
  • Follow all applicable laws during your testing.

3. What You Can Expect

When you report a valid vulnerability:

  • We will acknowledge your report within 72 hours.
  • We will investigate and validate the issue.
  • We will provide updates on our progress to fix it.
  • We may publicly thank you for your contribution, if you wish.

4. Scope

This policy applies to all ZemQue-owned domains, applications, and APIs. Vulnerabilities in third-party services integrated with ZemQue should be reported to the respective providers.

5. No Legal Action

If you follow this Responsible Disclosure Policy, we will not take legal action against you for your report.
